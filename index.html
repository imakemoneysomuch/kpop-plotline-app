KPOP Plotline is a desktop tool for the channel owner’s own YouTube uploads. It checks a prepared video and its metadata, confirms the connected channel, and manages publication times.

This is an information site for a private uploader. There is no public signup, online upload form, or customer subscription.

How data is handledTerms of use

One deliberate sequence

From a local file
to a channel.

  1. Connect the owner’s channel

    Google authorization lets the desktop tool identify the intended YouTube channel.

  2. Check the upload package

    Review the video, title, description, rights information, visibility, and timing.

  3. Upload and track

    Send the authorized upload to YouTube and keep a local queue record and receipt.

What the uploader does

Channel checks

Read the authorized channel’s identity and recent upload information to check the destination and avoid occupied publication slots.

Owner-directed uploads

Send a prepared file and its metadata to YouTube, including the visibility and scheduled publication time selected for the job.

Local records

Keep authorization credentials, queue state, file checksums, and upload receipts on the operator’s computer.
